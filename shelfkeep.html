ShelfKeep by Navaark

Keep your shelf. Keep your doors.

A weekly service that shows which store doors are at risk at the next reset, and what to do this week to keep them.

Why doors get dropped

The buyer watches velocity. That is sales per store per week. A door below the bar for weeks gets dropped at the reset. Most brands find out when the door is already gone.

ShelfKeep flags those doors early, while there is still time to act. In the sample, 87 of 1,240 doors are at risk. Average velocity is 42 against a bar of 35. There are 9 weeks to the next reset. That is 9 weeks to fix what can still be fixed.

Doors at risk, named

Every store below the velocity bar, with its trend and weeks of supply. No guessing which doors are in trouble.

Slow products, flagged

The products dragging velocity down, and where they are slow. So you fix the product, not the whole brand.

Actions for the week

A short list of what to do this week. Talk to the buyer. Fund a markdown. Stop shipping to the slowest stores.

How it works

01 · Pull

We pull your store data

We pull your store sales and inventory each week, from the channels you sell in.

02 · Flag

We flag the doors at risk

We flag the doors below the velocity bar and the slow products, store by store.

03 · Act

You get a weekly memo

You get a one page memo with the actions for the week, in time to use it.

No new software to learn. No dashboard to staff. The memo is the product.
