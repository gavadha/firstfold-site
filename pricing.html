Questions founders ask

How does pricing work?

Find the tier that matches your door count. Use the estimator above if you are not sure. On one short call we confirm your scope: which channels, which SKUs, how much history. Tell us which stores. We handle the setup for you. You then get one exact one-time price, fixed in writing, before you decide. No surprises after you say yes. If your size falls between tiers, we price by scope, not by logo. The work decides the price, not the size of your brand name.

Is ShelfKeep really included, or is it billed on its own?

Included. Every tier price on this page covers both the Vantage view and the ShelfKeep weekly memo. There is no separate ShelfKeep charge.

Is this a monthly price?

No. The tier prices are one-time, and 90 days of fixes are included in that price. After the 90 days, you can add an optional yearly service plan for $750 a year. It covers fixes if a data source changes or the pipeline breaks.

How does payment work?

Half to start, half when your view is live. No monthly fee.

What if my view is not live on time?

We return your first payment. The live date is agreed in writing before we start, so there is no argument about it.

What if I have more than 50,000 doors?

Then we talk. Above 50,000 doors, or more than 10 channels, the price is a custom quote based on your scope. You still get the exact number in writing before you decide.

How long does it take, and what do you need from us?

Emerging in 3 to 5 days, Growing in about a week, Scaled in 2 to 3 weeks, from the day we have your files. We need your sales exports for the last 52 weeks (26 weeks for Emerging), for each channel you sell in, and your product list with SKUs. If you would rather we pull the data for you, we set that up on the call.

Who is Vantage for?

Founder-led food, beverage and beauty brands selling in three or more channels, without a big data team of their own.
